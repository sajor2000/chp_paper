## Methods
We analyzed direct CHM geographic-condition-year records from 2019 through 2024. For the 2022-2024 geographic comparison, eligible direct tract measures were pooled as summed numerators divided by summed denominators before ranking. Missing and suppressed values were not imputed; direct tract disease values were never interpolated or aggregated. CHM-PLACES comparisons were descriptive and used condition-specific eligible tract frames.
