# ChicagoHealthMap Project Profile

Load only for ChicagoHealthMap/CAPriCORN manuscripts. The approved protocol, SAP, claim ledger, and frozen outputs override this profile.

## Measure and inference contract

At first mention in Abstract, Methods, Results, and Discussion, use **EHR-diagnosed proportion among observed CAPriCORN adults** when that remains the verified estimand. It is not population prevalence, disease burden, individual risk, or a rate.

Use associational and ecological language: `was associated with`, `differed`, `was spatially aligned with`, and `was discordant with`. Do not claim underdiagnosis, unmet need, access barriers, care quality, optimal allocation, intervention impact, or improved outcomes without separate evidence.

Keep missing, suppressed, unreliable, withheld, structural zero, and observed zero distinct. State coverage and reliability beside geographic findings. Comparator discordance is a measurement question, not proof that one source is correct.

## Manuscript sequence

- Introduction: local assessment problem; ascertainment/capture boundary; reproducibility gap; objective.
- Methods: design/sources; observed adults and geography; numerator/denominator/phenotype; public-source harmonization; reliability/suppression/missingness; prespecified analyses; ethics/reporting.
- Results: resource scale and eligibility; reliability/coverage; Case Study 1; mirrored Case Study 2; sensitivities.
- Discussion: resource finding; each case; joint lesson; conditional planning relevance; limitations; brief conclusion.

Do not draft empirical Results before the project authorization and evidence-freeze gates permit it.

## Main display plan

1. Table 1: resource, observed population, coverage, suppression, and reliability.
2. Figure 1: study geography, capture, missingness, reliability, and inclusion.
3. Figure 2: Case Study 1 pattern, estimate, uncertainty, and comparator concordance.
4. Figure 3: Case Study 2 in the same visual grammar.
5. Table 2: primary unadjusted and adjusted estimates, prespecified CI levels, diagnostics, and robustness classification.

Place schemas, selection governance, annual analyses, aligned mortality, provenance, full diagnostics, sensitivities, and FQHC/CBO planning demonstrations in the supplement.

## Planning language

Findings may identify questions for review and support conditional conversations with FQHCs and community-based organizations. They do not direct service placement or establish community need. Keep planning demonstrations separate from evaluated implementation outcomes.
