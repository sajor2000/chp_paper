# Scientific Rigor Contract

## Prespecification

Freeze hypotheses, estimands, variable roles, analysis populations, transformations, model families, diagnostics, missing-data handling, multiplicity, sensitivities, and reporting rules before outcome-guided model selection. Preserve every post-freeze change with date, rationale, evidence, impact, and approver.

A principal model must remain principal regardless of an observed coefficient, P value, diagnostic preference, or whether a sensitivity estimate looks more favorable. Diagnostics may trigger prespecified sensitivities, withholding, or a `model-sensitive` label, but not silent estimator promotion. A compact workbook is an operational handoff and must not contradict or weaken the controlling narrative SAP.

Observational SAP structure is supported by DEBATE (PMCID: PMC6902479). Pre-SPEC (PMCID: PMC7487509) supports explicit prespecification and transparent deviations. TARMOS (PMCID: PMC8168830) supports structured missing-data strategy. Estimand guidance (PMCID: PMC9389514) supports aligning the research objective, population, treatment/exposure conditions, outcome, intercurrent events when applicable, and population-level summary.

## Reporting

For observational studies use STROBE. For routinely collected health data add RECORD. When equity is central, use STROBE-Equity. Apply SAGER to sex/gender reporting, distinguishing sex, gender, and the actual source variable. Use the live target-journal instructions as the publication source of truth and record the access date.

## Minimum decisions

- design and inferential frame;
- population, eligibility, units, clusters, geography, and time;
- source provenance and data freeze;
- numerator/denominator and observation semantics;
- estimands and interpretation language;
- primary, secondary, descriptive, sensitivity, and exploratory families;
- covariate rationale and degrees-of-freedom constraints;
- functional form and model diagnostics;
- spatial/cluster dependence decision gates where relevant;
- missingness, suppression, denominator, coverage, and measurement-error robustness;
- multiplicity and selective-reporting protection;
- negative-control decision, including a justified decision not to use one;
- tables/figures and journal constraints;
- reproducibility, independent verification, decision/deviation logs, and freeze authorization.
