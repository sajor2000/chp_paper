# Claim Language Lexicon

## Required measure language

Use **EHR-diagnosed proportion among observed CAPriCORN adults** when that is the
verified estimand. Do not replace it with unqualified prevalence, burden, risk, or rate.

## Approved concrete and associational language

Prefer `included`, `estimated`, `compared`, `differed`, and `was associated`. Ecological
results require associational language and cannot be interpreted as individual-level
relations.

## Prohibited causal or intervention language

For this observational ecological study, prohibit: `cause`, `caused`, `causes`, `drive`,
`drives`, `drove`, `effect`, `effects`, `impact`, `impacts`, `impacted`, `improve`,
`improves`, `improved`, `lead`, `leads`, `led`, `prevent`, `prevents`, `prevented`,
`reduce`, `reduces`, and `reduced`.

## Unsupported superlatives

Do not use `actionable`, `comprehensive`, `first`, `novel`, `representative`, `robust`,
`scalable`, `unique`, or `validated` unless the claim ledger defines and supports the
term.

## Conditional planning boundary

Findings may formulate planning questions but do not establish need, access, capacity, underdiagnosis, optimal allocation, care improvement, or outcomes.

Comparator discordance is a measurement and interpretation problem, not proof that one
source is correct and another is wrong.
