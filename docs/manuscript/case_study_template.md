# Mirrored Case Study Template

Use these eight headings, in this order, for each S5-selected case. Until S5, label the
packet provisional. Until S7, record contracts and authorized inputs only; do not add
empirical statements, numerical fields, or result-oriented headings.

## Why this case

State the distinct scientific and informatics test and its outcome-blinded selection
authority.

## Prespecified estimand

Identify the exposure, outcome, geography, period, population, model, uncertainty
interval, and multiplicity status from the signed SAP.

## Eligibility and data quality

Identify the governed fields for analyzable areas and periods, suppression, reliability,
missingness, and exclusions.

## Pattern and comparator contract

Identify the Chicago Health Map diagnosed-proportion field followed by the
source-qualified public comparator and its nonequivalence boundary.

## Primary estimate contract

Identify the governed fields for eligible sample, unadjusted and adjusted estimates,
confidence interval, spatial diagnostic, model decision, and robustness classification.

## Supportive analyses contract

Identify analyses classified as secondary or exploratory and their Supplement
destinations.

## Interpretive boundary

Identify case-specific selection, measurement, ecological, temporal, and comparator
limitations.

## Platform lesson

Identify the bounded question about reliability, triangulation, or transfer across
phenotypes. Do not state an intervention effect.
